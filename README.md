# 🚪 Knock-Knock-V

**Knock Knock V** is a feature-rich social networking platform designed to help people connect, communicate, and build communities. Share your moments, join interactive rooms, chat with friends, discover new people, and engage in meaningful conversations—all in one app.

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Social Profile
- Public & Private Profiles
- Following & Followers
- User Activity

### 📰 Content Sharing
- Create & Share Posts
- Stories
- Personalized Feed

### 💬 Messaging
- One-to-One Chat
- Group Chat
- Real-Time Messaging

### 🏠 Rooms & Communities
- Community Rooms
- Public & Private Rooms
- Join Discussions
- Connect with Like-minded People

### 🌍 Discover
- Discover Screen
- Find New People
- Explore Trending Content

### ❓ Engagement
- Question of the Day (QOD)
- Community Discussions
- Social Interactions

## 🚀 Why Knock Knock V?

Knock Knock V brings social networking, messaging, and communities together in one seamless platform. Whether you're sharing your daily moments, chatting with friends, joining interest-based rooms, or discovering new creators, the app offers a fun and engaging social experience.
